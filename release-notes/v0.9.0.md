- A call in a meeting's chat that isn't on your calendar, such as an ad-hoc call between a series' meetings or a follow-up after one, is now captured and summarised as a meeting of its own, named after the chat. Its row reads "ad-hoc call". To find these, the app looks at your 25 most recently active Teams chats about once an hour (see Calls in your meeting chats)
- A quick call just before a meeting, up to 30 minutes before its start, is kept with it: the Transcript tab shows a Separate call divider and the summary mentions the separate sessions. A call that started earlier and ran into the meeting is kept too, and recurring meetings no longer miss either
- To find ad-hoc calls the app now lists your Teams chats. It keeps only each meeting chat's id and last-updated time, ignores 1:1 and group chats, and never reads message text
